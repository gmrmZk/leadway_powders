About LEADWAY POWDERS

Building better food and beverage opportunities.

Leadway Powders is a B2B food ingredient sourcing and solutions business focused on serving the evolving food-service and wholesale industries. We connect businesses with product sourcing and customized ingredient solutions.

Our business model is built around working with established food ingredient suppliers and manufacturing partners to provide customers with access to diverse product categories and development opportunities.

Every business has different objectives. A bubble tea shop may need consistent beverage ingredients, while a regional distributor may require competitive bulk supply and a diverse product portfolio. That is why we place our customers at the center of everything we do.
